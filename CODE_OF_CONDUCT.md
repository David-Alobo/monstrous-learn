# Code of conduct

This code applies everywhere the courses meet: this repository (issues, pull requests,
discussions), the Discord community, and live sessions.

## What we expect

- Be welcoming and patient. People join from many countries, backgrounds and levels of experience;
  every question asked in good faith is a good question.
- Be respectful when you disagree. Criticise ideas and code, never people.
- Help others learn: explain, point to the lesson, share what worked for you.
- Credit other people's work, and do not present it as your own. In the capstone and peer reviews,
  submit your own work and review others' fairly and kindly.
- Keep it on topic and safe for everyone: no spam, no advertising without permission.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discrimination of any kind, including on the basis of nationality,
  ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, age or level of experience.
- Sexual language or imagery, threats, or encouraging harm.
- Sharing other people's private information without their permission.
- Copying homework answers or capstone work, or helping others to do so during a cohort.

## What happens

Moderators may remove messages, issues or comments that break this code, and may warn, mute or
remove anyone who breaks it, from the repository, the Discord, or a cohort (including withholding a
certificate for copied work).

To report a problem, write to [contact@monstrous.com.ng](mailto:contact@monstrous.com.ng) or message
a moderator on Discord. Reports are handled in confidence.

This code draws on the principles of the Contributor Covenant (https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
